Your field guide to the bits & bobs ＋

The little listening station

A supported 64-bit Raspberry Pi, its power supply, a microSD card, a USB microphone and a network connection. Start indoors, where it’s easy to check everything.

The intention is headless setup: your AI connects from the Mac over SSH. A keyboard and monitor are only needed if you choose to access the Pi directly.

Before it can answer the door

Check the CanaKit’s card and instructions to see whether Raspberry Pi OS is already installed. If it isn’t—or Wi-Fi, a user account and SSH aren’t configured—your AI guides the Raspberry Pi Imager setup. You confirm the correct card before it is erased, then insert it and connect power.

The installer checks the Pi’s OS and architecture, microphone, location, time, BirdNET model and recognition service. The bundled BirdNET-Go model needs no paid API key. Optional AI features have their own keys later.

Once everyone has found their perch

The garden can be quiet.
The system should still be healthy.

Songsketch keeps bird activity separate from equipment health. Reports reach the server automatically; offline reports wait and can be downloaded. Routine recovery needs no AI key. Your local AI has the project’s repair instructions when something needs a closer look.

Before leaving it unattended, let your AI verify a real detection and recovery after a restart, while you check the microphone and display in their final positions.
