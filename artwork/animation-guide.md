# Animation guide

The latest verified writing from **Design bird painting concepts** is bundled intact in the [painting system guide](painting-system/references/system-guide.md). It is the full guide, not a prompt-only substitute. The [implementation reference](painting-system/references/implementation.md), [prompt recipes](painting-system/references/prompt-recipes.md) and [validation guide](painting-system/references/validation-and-scaling.md) complete the authoring instructions.

The approval benchmark remains the 75-second Robin. Preserve real deposited sketch, water, pigment and detailed marks; pressure-shaped strokes, local broad-to-fine ordering and meaningful late progress. Do not replace it with an erased cover or bitmap crossfade. Final art, registration, renderer, seeds and choreography together reproduce the result.

The original five completed production animations are in [animations/](animations/), with their exact hashes in [catalogue.json](catalogue.json). The full finished-art collection is a separate [reference library](index.html) and [registry](registry.json).

At this snapshot, the fork was actively authoring the remaining forty animations for portrait and shared-branch use. Their finished portrait assets do not establish animation readiness. The intended reuse is a prepared bird layer plus separate backdrop, perch and lettering, with a foot anchor for shared-branch placement. This is recorded scope, not a claim that all layers or modes have passed verification.

See [pending-animations/README.md](pending-animations/README.md) for the explicit completion manifest and staging gate. No active animation work was copied into the finished folder. The [import snapshot](import-snapshot.json) identifies the source thread, guide hashes and retained reference files.
