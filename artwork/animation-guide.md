# Animation guide

The latest verified writing from **Design bird painting concepts** is bundled intact in the [painting system guide](painting-system/references/system-guide.md). It is the full guide, not a prompt-only substitute. The [implementation reference](painting-system/references/implementation.md), [prompt recipes](painting-system/references/prompt-recipes.md) and [validation guide](painting-system/references/validation-and-scaling.md) complete the authoring instructions.

The approval benchmark remains the 75-second Robin. Preserve real deposited sketch, water, pigment and detailed marks; pressure-shaped strokes, local broad-to-fine ordering and meaningful late progress. Do not replace it with an erased cover or bitmap crossfade. Final art, registration, renderer, seeds and choreography together reproduce the result.

The original five completed production animations are in [animations/](animations/), with their exact hashes in [catalogue.json](catalogue.json). The full finished-art collection is a separate [reference library](index.html) and [registry](registry.json).

The later completed source contains all 45 individual animations. Forty new ones are now in the [review collection](review.html), with checkpoint controls and accepted-reference comparisons. Their shared renderer and per-species registration are imported from a stable completed snapshot; repeated inline PNGs resolve to the exact local reference files, and authored drawing marks are built locally instead of shipping optional caches. Each performance is 75 seconds with fluidity 0.78.

A subsequent user request reports fragmentation and insufficient clarity in some new final frames. These forty are therefore **completed, awaiting fidelity review**, not promoted to garden playback. [The review catalogue](review-catalogue.json) keeps their approval flags false; [the live catalogue](catalogue.json) retains the original five. The [animation snapshot](animation-review-snapshot.json) records both completion evidence and later feedback. A finished schedule is not proof of accepted likeness.

Active shared-branch composition is deliberately separate and was not imported. The source's reusable bird layer, backdrop, perch, lettering and foot anchor remain authoring capabilities; the connected application currently displays individual portraits.

See [pending-animations/README.md](pending-animations/README.md) for future completion manifests and staging. The earlier [reference import snapshot](import-snapshot.json) identifies the guide hashes and retained artwork. Original approved animation files, benchmarks and retained status graphics were not regenerated or changed.
