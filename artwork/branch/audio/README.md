# Bird sound library

45 locally packaged reference recordings. These are natural recordings, not live garden audio. Sources, individual recordists, licences and modifications are listed in CREDITS.csv and ../content.json, and embedded in each MP3. Retain those credits and the applicable licence when redistributing. Adapted CC BY-SA recordings retain their source licence; the credits identify its version per file.

Excerpts are at most 12 seconds, mono MP3, with adjusted loudness and soft fades. Playback speed and pitch are unchanged. Shorter recordings are not artificially looped.

The Pied Wagtail entry uses a Motacilla alba recording from Switzerland; the British yarrellii subspecies is unconfirmed. Great Spotted Woodpecker uses drumming. The Rook clip includes natural rookery ambience. Source identification and technical decoding have been reviewed; full listening on the intended speakers and microphone-feedback commissioning remain.

Listen at /static/bird-library.html while the local server is running.
