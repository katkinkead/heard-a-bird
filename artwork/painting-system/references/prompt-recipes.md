# Prompt recipes

These templates accompany the preserved art and renderer; they do not replace them. For new finished illustrations, use the more specific prompt and reference hierarchy in [the final-art system](final-art-system.md). Existing-species motion adaptations have been made; transfer of the new illustration prompt to additional species still requires review.

## Existing artwork: motion-only trial

Use this for a motion trial on an existing illustration. It does not ask for a new image.

```text
Use $birdsong-painting-system to adapt the approved 75-second Robin painting
progression to the existing [SPECIES] illustration at [SOURCE].

Keep that source illustration's pose, silhouette, colours, geometry, feather
decoration and detailed final composition. Use the approved Robin as the
material-and-motion benchmark, not as a bird shape.
Preserve the chosen reference's annotation treatment. If it has no live status,
do not automatically add the Robin's "Singing now" example.

Build a considered graphite opening, local water before pigment, pigment that
continues to disperse after contact, and long pressure-loaded strokes with
visible widening, fuller deposits and tapered lifts. Let anatomical regions
develop through overlapping broad, medium and fine passages. Settle the washes
before the sharp coloured-pencil and engraved feather finish.

Target 75 seconds with comparable perceptible progress across ten-second
intervals. Reserve a substantial, appropriate feather/structure region for the
last ten seconds. The ending must do more than refine pixels or add lettering.
Keep the background subordinate and the final image as detailed as the source.

Register this species' silhouette, anatomy, sketch paths, wet regions and focal
areas. Adapt the timing profile to that anatomy; do not reuse the Robin's
hard-coded coordinates or just accelerate the old 90-second animation.

Use the preserved procedural mark system. Do not uncover a finished bitmap
with brush masks, fade in whole body parts or generate unrelated stage images.
Keep one restrained variation in the gesture route if it helps this bird.
Show one playable study and inspect 0/10/20/30/40/50/60/65/70/75-second checkpoints.
Do not change the other birds or start Pi/server integration.
```

Fill the species and source explicitly from the actual accessible files. If the source is a collection sheet, use the registered crop and exclude neighbouring birds and captions. Do not assume a file path from this example exists.

## New finished bird: illustration prompt

Use the canonical [finished-art prompt](final-art-system.md#finished-art-prompt) and [flexible species brief](final-art-record.json). Inspect the original images and the Woodpigeon refinement comparison for material, graphic organisation and detail hierarchy. Use v2 as the preferred rendering reference; v1 is comparison-only. Keep verified species anatomy separate from artistic references.

The brief should leave genuine room to invent: background shape and number, composition, expression, colour relationships and detail placement are open unless the current user request settles them. No mandatory ornamental wing, fixed set of geometry choices or one-feature quota. Review whether the result has both collection kinship and individual character.

Separating exact lettering and live/history overlays from the generated source remains a proposed production arrangement. For a faithful edit, preserve the existing source's annotations unless asked to change them.

## Compact motion vocabulary

Use: **composed graphite gesture; long tapered arrival; eased press and lift; loaded brush belly; local wet-on-wet dispersal; pigment settling; broken dry-brush edge; paper tooth; directional feather flow; overlapping anatomical passages; selective glazing; crisp coloured-pencil finish; engraved feather accents; perceptually balanced pacing; meaningful late construction.**

Avoid using “watercolour reveal” or “brush wipe” as shorthand. They encourage the rejected mask effect. “Random”, “magical”, “cute”, “whimsical” and “surprise me” without constraints invite stylistic drift.

## Prompt for a controlled variation

```text
Keep the accepted [SPECIES] source, material language, 75-second duration and
detailed finish. Change only [ONE GESTURE ROUTE / ORDER OF TWO INDEPENDENT
REGIONS / PIGMENT ENTRY POINT]. Retain water-before-pigment and local
broad→medium→fine order. Reserve a visible structural feature for the ending.
Record the seed and variant. Check the change against the approved baseline
at normal viewing size, especially 60→70 and 70→75 seconds.
```

Do not apply a variation to the approved baseline in place. Save a candidate so it can be compared.
