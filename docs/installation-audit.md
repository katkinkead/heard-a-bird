# Installation audit — 22 October 2026

The indoor MVP is ready for physical commissioning, not certified unattended production. The overall wiring is plausible. The outdoor assembly still needs a dimensioned mounting/sealing/thermal design, and two requested software behaviours remain incomplete. This audit read live Canva page 1, repository implementation and primary hardware documentation. No Canva objects or physical equipment were changed.

## Enclosure and cable fit

[Hammond's current 1590Z150 page](https://www.hammfg.com/part/1590Z150) gives approximately **221 × 120 × 81 mm external** dimensions and a maximum PCB footprint of approximately **207 × 106 mm**. Its naming of PCB length/width is transposed relative to the enclosure axes; these are not a promise of a completely unobstructed rectangular cavity. Older catalogue dimensions differ; use the drawing for the actual supplied revision. The kit's plastic case is not included in this preliminary footprint assessment.

The [Pi 5 board](https://datasheets.raspberrypi.com/rpi5/raspberry-pi-5-mechanical-drawing.pdf) is approximately **85 × 56 mm**; the [Waveshare splitter](https://www.waveshare.com/product/accessories/power-heat-sinks/poe-splitter-25w-type-c.htm) is **102.5 × 29.6 × 24.8 mm**. Their bare footprints can plausibly fit side by side in the Hammond. End-to-end they consume about 187.5 mm of the roughly 207 mm mounting length, leaving only about 19.5 mm before allowing plugs, walls and routing: do not approve a straight inline arrangement from those numbers alone.

The layout must include UGREEN's body and attached lead, two Ethernet connections at the splitter, the Pi's Ethernet and USB plugs, its USB-C power bend, cooler height, standoffs, SD-card access, lid/gasket clearance, gland locknuts and tool access. Reserve actual manufacturer cable bend radii and boot lengths; do not invent a generic clearance and call it verified. Mount electronics mechanically, clear of conductive box surfaces. Use a removable mounting plate/internal bosses where appropriate rather than unnecessary penetrations through the weather barrier. If retaining the entire CanaKit plastic case, re-check the layout and cooling independently.

### Microphone entry

[OBO 2024940 / V-TEC TB20](https://kz.obo-bettermann.com/out/media/VBS-V-TEC_TB20-2024940-en.pdf) is an **M20 × 1.5 split gland with a closed insert**, not a pre-sized microphone seal. It supports preterminated cables, but the blank insert must be prepared for the actual jacket diameter. Its approximately 33.5 mm outer envelope and 10 mm thread also require wall, seal and locknut clearance. [OBO's instructions](https://www.obo.pl/out/media/01-130_MA%20Teilbare%20Kabelverschraubung%20V%20TEC%20TB20_DE_EN_ES_RU.pdf) distinguish the blank part from pre-sized 6–8 mm and larger inserts. The Clippy cable diameter has not been established for the ordered assembly. Do not substitute a 6–8 mm insert merely because it is also M20, or pack a loose seal with tape. Check the enclosure-to-gland seal as well as the cable seal.

### Ethernet entry

The [board's Pi Hut RJ45 item](https://thepihut.com/products/cable-gland-waterproof-rj-45-ethernet-connector) is a **panel feedthrough with two sockets**, not merely a hole that passes the existing cable. It therefore needs a short internal Ethernet patch cable to the splitter. The same design's [Adafruit documentation](https://www.adafruit.com/product/827) warns that moulded boots can obstruct assembly and provides dimensional drawings. Its published IP67 rating does not establish a particular PoE+ current rating, cable seal range or shield continuity for our chosen cable. Confirm these or choose a documented PoE+ feedthrough; alternatively use a correctly sized split gland and continuous cable to remove an extra electrical join.

The outdoor Cat6 model and length remain **TBD in Canva**. Before purchase specify full-copper conductors, outdoor/UV suitability (and wet/underground suitability if buried), jacket outside diameter, bend radius, connector dimensions, shield/earthing arrangement and total channel length. “Cat6” alone does not answer these questions. Seal the jacket, not a plug boot; use bottom entries and drip loops. No final cable-to-gland fit is verified yet.

## Weather and temperature

[Hammond](https://www.hammfg.com/electronics/small-case/diecast/1590z) rates the unmodified enclosure series for water/dust ingress protection and supplies the gasket loose. Fit that gasket correctly. Drilled entries, mounting hardware, cable seals and the microphone determine the completed assembly's protection; component IP ratings cannot certify the assembled station. Mount under shelter and out of direct sun. The [Puffer Urchin](https://micbooster.com/product/puffer-urchin-clippy-windshield/) is specified as fur/foam wind protection, not a waterproof membrane. The external microphone needs a rain-sheltered acoustic opening with drainage, not an airtight box that muffles the birds.

Day/night changes can cause internal condensation even when rain cannot enter. Decide on a correctly mounted, suitably rated membrane pressure-compensation device; Hammond offers [pressure/ventilation accessories](https://www.hammfg.com/electronics/small-case/diecast/1590z). A breather can assist pressure/moisture management but does not guarantee a dry interior or replace thermal design. Do not drill an unprotected ventilation hole. Any chosen vent needs its own dimensions, rating and mounting instructions; no vent has been selected in this audit.

**Using the Hammond as a heatsink is feasible, but is not yet designed.** [ARCTIC TP-3](https://www.arctic.de/en/TP-3/ACTPD00058A) is electrically insulating and explicitly non-adhesive. The board's 1.5 mm choice is not evidence that the CPU-to-wall gap is 1.5 mm. It must not be used to glue the board in place or bridge an arbitrary gap by stacking pads. A conductive block/spreader needs controlled contact pressure and independent board mounting without bending the PCB or contacting unintended components. A plastic Pi case prevents this direct path unless deliberately redesigned. A fan inside a sealed enclosure moves heat to internal air; heat still has to leave through the enclosure.

Cooling acceptance should use the actual closed box, splitter and BirdNET workload at the worst expected shaded ambient temperature, including restart after a warm power interruption. Measure ambient/internal temperature alongside CPU temperature, inference throughput and throttle/undervoltage flags. Our existing 75°C warning is a provisional operational threshold, not a thermal certification. [Raspberry Pi documents throttling from 80°C](https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/heating-and-cooling-raspberry-pi-5/); retain margin below that rather than treating throttling as acceptable cooling. A 24–48 hour soak is a useful first screen, not proof of all-season life. The metal box may also reduce Wi-Fi reception; production should use the splitter's wired Ethernet.

## Power and audio

The injector/splitter standards match and the proposed Pi plus audio load is plausible. [Production power notes](production.md) retain the actual limits and conditional `usb_max_current_enable=1` helper. The warning means a 5 A supply was not recognised, not simply that a non-Pi brand or USB-C was used. USB boot and USB accessory current are separate from booting our microSD installation. Do not enable a current override solely to hide an actual voltage fault.

The newer **UACC-ETH-SP-Pro** is the recommended replacement candidate for the unavailable seller link, with [manufacturer outdoor/PoE specifications](https://techspecs.ui.com/unifi/accessories/uacc-eth-sp-pro) and a [UK listing](https://www.netxl.com/poe-injectors/ubiquiti-uacc-eth-sp-pro-surge-protection-outdoor/). The retrieved manufacturer table says generic PoE, not an explicit 802.3at current limit; keep confirmation of the complete load/grounding arrangement open. A surge device needs proper bonding/earthing and correct placement; plugging one into Ethernet alone does not complete the protection design. Keep mains/injector indoors. An installer should settle the building-entry and remote-end protection arrangement; do not improvise earth rods or mains-ground connections.

UGREEN/Clippy has real precedent, but electrical noise, gain, wind and fan noise still need acceptance checks. Compare quiet recordings on the kit mains supply and the final PoE arrangement; a loud phone recording alone cannot prove faint-bird sensitivity. See [audio research](birdnet-pi-reliability.md). Do not add USB-over-Ethernet extenders to this design: the planned long run carries standard PoE networking, with the USB adapter beside the Pi.

## Communications and operations audit

The implemented path is microphone → BirdNET-Go on Pi → local authenticated-history relay → Songsketch HTTP on the indoor server → browser SSE/state plus local animation assets. BirdNET's container API is published on Pi loopback, not automatically to the whole LAN. Pi audio is not sent to Songsketch; only identities, timestamps, health and reports are transferred.

- **LAN reachability:** reserve addresses or verify stable names, permit the required host firewall traffic, avoid guest Wi-Fi client isolation, and pair the Pi to the server's reachable address rather than `localhost`. A switch distributes traffic but is not a replacement for a router/DHCP service. The Mac must remain awake and connected. These are deployment checks, not reasons to disable the firewall wholesale.
- **Recovery:** relay history/outbox, report deduplication, retry backoff and server-owned queue are implemented and exercised. Recovery depends on retained BirdNET history and available disk. Loss while the microphone/Pi itself is off is unrecoverable. Check clock synchronisation and `Europe/London`; wrong/future clocks can invalidate records or distort recency.
- **Remote viewing:** [private Tailscale access](remote-viewing.md) is documented but not installed or verified. “Accessible from anywhere” is not the default LAN installation. Do not port-forward the application publicly. LAN HTTP is unencrypted; use a trusted network or encrypted tunnel.
- **Display:** the Canva Amazon embed is a **Samsung bezel accessory**, not a purchased screen. The screen/browser player is still TBD. A normal monitor needs an HDMI-connected computer; a smart-TV browser needs actual testing. Modern JavaScript support, preparation time, animation performance, kiosk startup and sleep/reconnect behaviour are unresolved. The current display follows the server clock, so cold preparation can skip opening strokes. Full 75-second review playback in the gallery does not certify live playback on a TV.
- **Test-data gap:** `pi/relay.py:normalize_detection` sets `test=False`. Playing a phone bird near the real microphone will be counted as a normal detection, unlike the dashboard's explicitly labelled test painting. A real-audio commissioning session needs durable tagging that remains correct through delayed delivery/replay. Do not fix this by resetting real history.
- **Browser reporting gap:** `static/display.js` discards a failed pending frame or preparation timeout and keeps the old image. It does not post those errors or retain them for later upload. Pi fault reporting is implemented; renderer reporting is a separate incomplete requirement.
- **Email:** SMTP settings and provider connectivity are needed, not just Monty's recipient address. Unit tests and simulator inboxes are not external delivery evidence. A real local SMTP transport and eventual provider/inbox check remain required.
- **Storage/maintenance:** use endurance-rated Pi storage and explicit recording retention. Server snapshots on the same drive are not an off-device backup. Linux service installation, migration/restore and automatic power recovery need clean-host tests. The restore CLI currently also probes global port 8765 before honouring a separate target directory, so a healthy unrelated Songsketch instance blocks an isolated restore rehearsal; this is an audit finding, not a reason to stop the real server. An offline server cannot send its own outage alert. Optional AI diagnosis returns advice; it is not an autonomous arbitrary repair agent.

## Evidence from this audit

- **242 application tests passed** on macOS/Python 3.9.6 with loopback access. The initial sandboxed run could not bind local sockets (40 errors); the authorised local-network rerun passed. This was an environment restriction, not 40 product regressions.

[Sanitized run evidence](qa-evidence/2026-10-22/system-audit-scenarios.json). No real garden data, SMTP provider, AI service or Pi was contacted. The temporary preview is not a persistent installed service.
- The [earlier real ARM64 BirdNET run](birdnet-live-validation.md) remains valid evidence for its recorded audio/API/relay checks; it was not repeated or upgraded into a hardware claim by this audit.

The next engineering work is the dimensioned outdoor assembly and the two software gaps above, followed by the [remaining release-validation matrix](release-validation.md). Keep the indoor phone-bird MVP separate from outdoor commissioning.
