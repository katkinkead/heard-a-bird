## Grading criteria

- Meaningful value for intended people — 30%. Practical, educational, emotional or creative benefit; audience size is not a proxy. Highest anchor: clearly identified audience and substantial value matched to its context.
- Originality and purposeful craft — 25%. Distinctive, careful choices that advance the purpose; complexity, equipment and polish alone do not earn credit.
- Tangible result and evidence of delivery — 30%. Readily experienceable work and credible, form-appropriate evidence. Separate demonstrated outcomes from future claims.
- Thoughtful iteration — 15%. Consequential refinement informed by feedback, trials or deliberate decisions; judge improvement, not hours spent.

## Audience and language decisions

Audience: anyone with a backyard, garden or outdoor space; not specifically gardeners or experienced birdwatchers. Iteration about the branch is “designing for multiple birds on screen,” not “designing for the whole garden.” Regional shared observations are a future opt-in direction, not an implemented network or proven population-monitoring outcome.

## Findings

The opening “a beautiful landscape can still look beautiful, even while it’s losing life” provides a clear reason to care. The home/display visual makes the experience understandable. The seven-minute walkthrough spends substantial attention on explanation and future hardware; the executable painting and iteration evidence should therefore be prominent, separately accessible links.

Fix or clarify:

1. Around 0:52, the exposed transcript says “73 million fewer bird species.” Check the spoken line and captions. The intended BTO statistic is fewer individual birds, not species. Source: https://www.bto.org/our-work/news/press/73-million-birds-gone-1970-which-have-vanished-near-you
2. Around 2:28, narration compares 18 “times” with 9 “days.” The screen uses days. Use the same metric and comparable listening coverage; keep sample values labelled illustrative.
3. Label regional/network screens as a future concept and sample data. Recorded acoustic activity alone is not a population count or evidence that the product reverses decline.
4. Around 5:31 onward, the custom PCB is described as designed. Distinguish design files and engineering checks from fabricated/bench-tested hardware. The board shown may represent newer work in another thread; no claim is made that it is merely a render or that no design exists.
5. Around 6:43, the quoted approximately $50 per board needs an estimate basis: quantity, included assembly/module/mic and excluded enclosure, supply, shipping and tax as applicable. Do not imply a verified complete retail-device cost.
